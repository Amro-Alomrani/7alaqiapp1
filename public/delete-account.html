Delete a 7alaqi account and data

7alaqi customers and barbers can request deletion here without reinstalling the app.

  1. Select “Continue to deletion”.
  2. Sign in to the account you want deleted. Use the password-reset link on the sign-in page if needed.
  3. Confirm account and data deletion. You may need to sign in again if your verification session has expired.

When the request completes, 7alaqi's deletion process removes the Firebase account, customer or barber profile, associated data and content, and uploaded files. Deletion is permanent.

If you cannot sign in or the request fails, contact support at 7alaqi.suuport@gmail.com.
